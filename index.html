SERVICES

You retain control.

We handle the detail.

Initial valuation and assessment of the property

In-depth research and local estate agent appraisals to establish market value, with advice on presentation and works to increase saleability. We also have a panel of auctioneers should the property be suitable for auction and can manage that process on your behalf, with key decisions on pricing made by you.

A fully managed house sale service

A single point of contact with updates at all key milestones, weekly reporting and monthly reviews. On your behalf we manage estate agent performance, qualifying offers in full and progressing the sale through to completion.

Emergency and planned property maintenance

A range of services to keep vacant property safe, secure and well maintained, with a national panel of approved contractors and trusted specialists. We provide competitive quotations with costs agreed in advance, with full photographic evidence provided on completion of work.

Insurance compliant property inspections

We conduct weekly inspections to ensure your property remains secure, well-maintained and compliant with insurance requirements.

Specialist surveys and property reports

We provide a comprehensive range of specialist surveying services and reports, including RICS valuations for IHT purposes, structural surveys, drainage surveys, wall tie reports, and other specialist property services.

Essential costs carried for the estate

We will carry all reasonable costs until the estate has been encashed, interim funds are available or the property is sold. We understand that probate is not a linear process.
