Initial valuation and assessment of the property

In-depth research and local estate agent appraisals to establish market value, with advice on presentation and works to increase saleability.

Emergency and planned property maintenance

A range of services to keep vacant property safe, secure and well maintained — with a panel of vetted contractors across the UK and all necessary insurances in place.

Insurance compliant property inspections

Weekly key-holder inspections to satisfy insurance requirements, with reporting on any vandalism or remedial works. Additional vacant property inspections available via our contractor panel.

Chattels valuation, clearance and removals

Competitive quotations with costs agreed in advance. Full photographic evidence provided on completion of all work.

Specialist surveys and property reports
